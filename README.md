# THE ARCHIVE

> **Some files were never meant to be opened.**

**THE ARCHIVE** is a 2D narrative mystery game built around investigation, hidden messages, puzzles, unreliable information, and a story that connects across multiple seasons.

You are not simply playing through a story.

You are collecting evidence.

You are questioning messages.

You are connecting events.

And sometimes...

**you have to decide what is real.**

---

## 🗂️ THE STORY

A mysterious digital archive appears.

Nobody knows who created it.

Nobody knows why it exists.

Inside it are documents, recordings, images, conversations, strange files, and information that should not exist.

Some files seem to describe events from the past.

Some seem to describe events that have not happened yet.

And some appear to be connected directly to **you**.

The deeper you investigate, the less certain the truth becomes.

### Four connected seasons.

### One growing mystery.

### Countless unanswered questions.

The story is designed as a connected narrative graph, meaning clues discovered in one season may completely change the meaning of something you saw much earlier.

Nothing is guaranteed to be what it first appears to be.

---

# 🧩 INVESTIGATE

THE ARCHIVE is built around investigation.

You may encounter:

- suspicious files
- unknown senders
- corrupted records
- hidden messages
- puzzles
- audio clues
- visual clues
- evidence
- strange timestamps
- contradictory information
- secrets hidden inside the game

Some messages may have no known sender.

Some information may be incomplete.

And sometimes the most important clue is the thing you almost ignored.

# 🔍 THE FOUR SEASONS

The game is divided into four connected seasons.

**Season I — THE ARCHIVE**

**Season II — THE WITNESS**

**Season III — THE OBSERVER**

**Season IV — THE LAST RECORD**

They are not four separate stories.

They are four parts of the same mystery.

A clue from one season may become important much later.

A character you thought you understood may look very different after another discovery.

And something you thought was an answer may turn out to be another question.
