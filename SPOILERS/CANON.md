# THE ARCHIVE — Locked Canon (v1.0)

Do not change this file without explicit approval.

## What the Archive is

The Archive is not a building that stores paper. It is the **Halcyon Memory Protocol**: a classified system that stores human memory as structured records. It was housed at **Halcyon Records Facility**, **Vesper Wing**, access node **17-A**.

The protocol stores time as a **directed graph**, not a line. A "season" is a neighbourhood of that graph. Clues are edges.

The facility is closed. The terminal still answers.

## The player

The player is granted access as a replacement archivist. This is the cover story of Season I. It is not the whole truth.

## The central mystery (locked)

Who is the player?

- S I — THE ARCHIVE suggests a new hire.
- S II — THE WITNESS suggests a subject under observation, and Nora as witness to the first ingest.
