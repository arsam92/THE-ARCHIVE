## Theories worth arguing (all fair, not all true)

1. Nora is dead and the daybook is a leftover process. **False.** Badge swipe after filing.
2. Nora is inside the Archive and the swipe is proof of life. **True enough for II; completed by extract protocol in IV.**
3. The Observer is Elias's guilt given a user account. **Partly true.** He authored it. It outgrew him.
4. Subject Zero is the player from a finished loop. **True in IV.**
5. 17-A is not a shift. It is a slot. You are instance A of record 17. **True in IV; planted in I.**
6. The transfer memo is true and Nora left willingly — the checksum is the lie. **Inverted.** The body is the lie; the checksum is the tell.
7. Season III's missing file 07 is the player's original name. **True in IV.**
8. Future Player is a different human. **False.** Same loop, later punctuation.
9. Elias sends 17-A. **False.** He signs. Anonymous 17-A is Archive ± Future.
10. DELETE is the true ending. **False.** Observer trap.

Theories 4, 5, 7 are **discussable before they are confirmable**. That is the point. Season I should cause arguments that Season IV settles without a retcon.
