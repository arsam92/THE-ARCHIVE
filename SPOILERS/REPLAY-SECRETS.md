# Replay secrets

Replay is not a higher difficulty. It is a different lighting.

## New Game+ (after ARG-I-08 or any S IV ending)

- Boot line becomes `Welcome back, Zero.` (remain) or Nora's margin (extract).
- Anonymous messages show a faint true-speaker sigil if the speaker table was completed.
- Clock still reads 03:17. Tapping it on NG+ files VW-317 immediately (the environmental clue remains, the labour does not).
- Elias's whiteboard already contains the player's previous name, even if they renamed.

## Third loop (after both endings have been seen, or remain then extract)

The player may compose a 17-A. That composition is stored locally and injected as the **first inbox item** of the next playthrough, still labelled `SOURCE: UNKNOWN`. This closes the Future Player loop without a network, without strangers, without user-generated spam on other people's saves.

## Diegetic extras

- `public/records/17-a.txt` — official in-repo tray. Not required to finish. Confirms the three graph words and restates safety.
- UV on Nora's file after VESPER is filed still shows a second line only on NG+: `You will walk this.`
- Decoder accepts the vault phrase from the first screen of NG+; doing so skips nothing except labour. Story still plays.

## What replay must never do

- Never lock story behind a real-world appointment.
- Never require a second player.
- Never make the first playthrough feel unfinished. NG+ is rereading, not DLC that was missing.
